EON Home Assistant

Privacy Policy

This policy describes the use of Google user data by EON Home Assistant, a personal integration operated by the mailbox owner.

Access and use

The integration requests read-only access to the dedicated Gmail account selected during Google authorization. It uses message headers and matching E.ON spreadsheet attachments to identify and import electricity consumption. The Gmail permission applies to the authorized mailbox.

Storage and retention

The operator keeps the OAuth credentials and imported measurement data on systems they control. Home Assistant stores the resulting statistics under its configured retention and backup policies. The operator can remove the integration’s stored credentials and data and delete corresponding local backups.

Sharing

The integration uses Google user data for this import. It does not sell that data, use it for advertising, or send it to an external analytics service.

Revoke access or contact the operator

The mailbox owner can revoke access in their Google Account connections. For questions or deletion requests, contact the support address shown on the Google consent screen.
